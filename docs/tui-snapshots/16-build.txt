  A preset says what to check. A baseline is a preset plus the values you expect, sealed.

  1. Start from a preset: copy presets/example-tenant-hygiene.json into
     ~/M365BaselineCheck/presets/ and edit it. CLAUDE.md explains every field.
  2. Draft a baseline: reads this tenant and writes what it finds as the expected values.
  3. Review the draft, change any value you do not want to keep, then seal it. Only sealed
     baselines run.

  Keep your own presets and baselines in ~/M365BaselineCheck or a private repository, never in
  this public one.
